    How counts work

    A PR counts once per day or week, even if you review it again. Weekly totals count distinct PRs, so they can be lower than the sum of the days. Submitted GitHub reviews include comment-only reviews; draft reviews, ordinary comments and AI runs are excluded. Hidden inbox PRs still count here. History covers repositories your GitHub login can access.

    The activity trend averages daily counts over the last 5 or 10 complete Monday–Friday workdays, excluding marked time off. Zero-activity workdays count. Weekend and time-off activity stays in the bars but is excluded from the average. The line holds steady across excluded days and starts once a full window is available. Today and the last synced day are excluded until a later sync confirms their full activity. Trends follow the report repository filter. Weekly bars and comparisons remain distinct-PR totals, without a daily-average overlay.
